# Loopfield Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 4, 2026

Loopfield is developed by zhang tao (Daftlamb).

## Data in the app

Loopfield does not require an account. We do not operate a server that receives your projects or usage data. The app does not include advertising, analytics SDKs, or tracking. Projects, preferences and instrument states are stored locally on your device. They may be included in device backups managed by your operating system or backup provider.

## Files and sharing

Import and export happen only when you choose them. When you share a project or audio file, the destination app or service you select handles that file under its own policies. Deleting the app removes its local data; separately exported files and backups must be deleted separately.

## Third-party instruments

You may optionally load installed AUv3 instruments. Those instruments are supplied by other apps, may process audio and MIDI, and have their own privacy policies. Loopfield stores the plugin state needed to restore your project on your device. This policy does not cover another developer's app or plug-in.

## Support

If you email us, we receive the information you include and use it to answer your request. We do not sell your information. Avoid sending sensitive personal data. Apple may process purchase information or diagnostics according to your Apple settings and Apple's privacy policy; Loopfield does not receive your payment details.

## Contact and changes

Questions or requests: daftlamb@gmail.com. Updates to this policy will be posted here with a revised effective date.
